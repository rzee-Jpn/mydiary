Europe Illustrated.

Its Picturesque Scenes and Places of Note.

FROM ORIGINAL DRAWINGS MADE IN
FRANCE, THE PYRENEES, BELGIUM, AND ON
THE RHINE,

By the most Eminent Artists, among whom are TURNER,
ALLOM, BARTLETT, LEITCH, &c.

ENGRAVED ON STEEL IN THE HIGHEST STYLE
OF ART.

The New Edition will be printed on good paper, and be Illustrated with at least One Hundred and Twenty Steel Engravings.

This Work treats of the most picturesque parts of Europe, and it is not too much to say the Illustrations carry with them a beauty, an interest, and a value which no production in the realms of Art and Literature has as yet equalled, far less surpassed.
