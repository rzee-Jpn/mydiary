Object.III.

That if this Method will hold, the intrinsick Value of Money may be but half as little as I propose, and the Subject will be always in fear of farther Alterations.

114Answ. This I confess would be a very material Objection against Mr. Lound’s Method, but hath no force in ours, because the Government saves nothing by lessening the intrinsick Value; for what it saves at present, it must pay Interest for, and Principal too at the long run; besides, the Government aims at nothing but to rectifie the Coin, and keep it from being imbezil’d.
