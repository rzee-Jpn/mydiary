Object.IV.

The Money of England will be too much for 300 Men to receive in one Day.

Answ. It is but 10000 l. a Man one with another; i. e. but 100 hundred Pound-bags, which may very well be weighed and seal’d up, and entered in a Book in 5 hours time; viz. 20 in an hour; and as for the great Banks and Cashes of England, such as the Royal Bank, East-India Company, and the like, they need not remove 115their Money at all; but Commissioners may be sworn and sent to their respective Treasuries, taking sufficient Security of the said Treasurers and Cashiers, that none of the said Money shall be remov’d or touch’d in 24 hours.
